One system. Every capability.

ORIAN combines an agent operating system, private AI, trading intelligence and automation into a single desktop app.

🧠

Neural OS Agents

60+ specialised agents handle goals, coding, self-repair, scheduling and more — orchestrated automatically.

🎙️

Voice-First Control

Talk to ORIAN. It listens, reasons and acts — cloneable voices, local TTS, no cloud required.

🔒

Local-First Privacy

Chat history, notes, credentials and files stay on your computer. Local LLMs via Ollama process everything on-device.

📈

Trading Intelligence

Integrated TBQ engine + Vision AI on MT5 — signals, multi-pair correlation, risk management and KPIs.

📧

Google & Gmail

Read and send email, manage calendar and tasks — only with your explicit OAuth consent, tokens encrypted locally.

⚙️

Full Device Control

Voice or chat can operate your PC — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, volume, brightness, displays, power states.

🧩

Skills & Evolution

Installable skills, self-learning pipelines and an evolution engine that proposes and tests its own improvements.

🛠️

Self-Healing

Integrity checks, auto-restart and graceful degradation keep the system running even when parts fail.

Your data never leaves your machine.

ORIAN is a personal, locally-run application. It connects to third-party services only when you explicitly authorise them, never sells or shares personal data, and stores tokens encrypted with your OS keychain.
