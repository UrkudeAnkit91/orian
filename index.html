Modules // 01

One system. Every capability.

An agent operating system, private AI, trading intelligence and automation — fused into a single desktop application.

Neural OS Agents

60+ specialised agents handle goals, coding, self-repair and scheduling — orchestrated automatically.

Voice-First Control

Talk to ORIAN. It listens, reasons and acts — local speech recognition and TTS, no cloud required.

Local-First Privacy

Chat history, notes, credentials and files stay on your machine. Local LLMs via Ollama process everything on-device.

Trading Intelligence

Integrated TBQ engine + Vision AI on MT5 — signals, multi-pair correlation, risk management and KPIs.

Google & Gmail

Read and send email, manage calendar and tasks — only with your explicit OAuth consent, tokens encrypted locally.

Full Device Control

Voice or chat can operate your PC — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, volume, brightness, displays and power states.

Skills & Evolution

Installable skills, self-learning pipelines and an evolution engine that proposes and tests its own improvements.

Self-Healing

Integrity checks, auto-restart and graceful degradation keep the system running even when parts fail.
